About Who's Tha Best

Empowering diners and restaurant owners through honest feedback

🎯

Our Mission

Who's Tha Best is dedicated to creating a transparent platform where food lovers can share their genuine experiences with specific menu items, not just restaurants. We believe that every dish deserves its own spotlight, and every diner's opinion matters.

✨

What Makes Us Different

  • Item-Specific Reviews: Rate individual dishes and drinks, not just the venue
  • Owner Insights: Restaurant owners can see real-time feedback and make improvements
  • Visual Experience: Share your food photography and inspire others
  • Smart Menu Parsing: Upload menus and let AI extract items automatically
👥

For Diners

Discover the best dishes in your area, share your culinary adventures, and help others make informed dining decisions. Your reviews help create a community of food enthusiasts who value quality and authenticity.

🏪

For Restaurant Owners

Get direct feedback on your menu items, understand what customers love (and what needs work), and make data-driven decisions to improve your offerings. See your ratings grow as you respond to customer feedback.

🚀

Our Vision

We envision a world where every great dish gets the recognition it deserves, and where restaurant owners have the insights they need to continuously improve. Together, we're building a more transparent and delicious food industry.
